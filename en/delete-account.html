Delete your Trackbook data

Trackbook has no accounts. Your notebook lives on your phone and, if you switched on iCloud sync, in your private iCloud. You delete both yourself; there is no need to write to us.

On your phone

Open Settings and tap Clear notebook, or delete the app. Either deletes the notebook on that device. What Trackbook wrote to Apple Health or Health Connect can be removed by the app when you switch the Health switch off; otherwise you manage it there. Exported files and your phone's own backups (iCloud or Google) you delete where they are kept.

In iCloud (iPhone and iPad)

Switch off iCloud sync in Trackbook's Settings. The app then offers to delete the iCloud copy for all your devices.

Without the app, use the iOS Settings app: [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage, then choose Trackbook and delete its data (the exact menu names vary between iOS versions). Your iCloud is yours; Trackbook has no access to it, so it cannot delete it for you.
